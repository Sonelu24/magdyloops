# Privacy Policy for MagdyLoops

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

**MagdyLoops** ("the App") is a knitting and crochet row counter, project organizer, and yarn calculator application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to being completely transparent about how the App handles your information.

---

## 1. No Personal Data Collection or Transmission

**MagdyLoops operates 100% offline.**
- We do **not** collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data on external servers.
- We do **not** require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address or phone number.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage on Your Device

All information you enter into **MagdyLoops** is stored **exclusively locally on your device** in the App's private internal storage and SQLite database, including:
- The display name and craft preference (Knitting, Crochet, or Both) you enter during onboarding.
- Your project names, row/round counts, needle/hook sizes, yarn details, and pattern notes.
- Pattern plans and progress photos that you choose to attach to your projects.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device using your operating system's standard device backup features.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

The App requests only the minimum system capabilities required for its features:
- **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)**: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when incrementing or decrementing row and round counters.
- **Android System Photo Picker**: When you choose to attach a pattern plan or progress photo to a project, the App uses Android's built-in Photo Picker so you can select specific images without granting the App broad access to your entire photo library.

---

## 4. Data Deletion & User Control

You have full control over your data at all times:
- You can edit or delete any individual project or attached photo directly within the App.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings permanently deletes all locally stored projects, counts, preferences, and photos from your device.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because **MagdyLoops** does not collect or transmit any personal information from any user of any age, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of the App, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

---

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or **MagdyLoops**, please contact us by opening an issue on our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/Sonelu24/magdyloops.
